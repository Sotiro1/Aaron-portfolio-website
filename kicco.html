Prototype

Kicco finance dashboard

A finance dashboard prototype for Kicco Espresso, my family's business. It brings revenue, costs, labour, suppliers, budgets and GST for every store into one place, with an AI panel you can ask questions about the numbers.

Client
Kicco Espresso
Type
Web app prototype
Status
End-to-end prototype

Preview

What it covers

Overview across stores

Totals for revenue, costs, labour and net revenue, filterable by store and by 7 days, 30 days, month or all time.

Trends and recent activity

A monthly trends chart compares revenue, costs and labour, next to a feed of the latest transactions.

Budgets and forecasting

Separate sections for suppliers, budgets, benchmarks, budget vs actual, forecasts and GST.

AI insights

An insights panel built on the OpenAI API that answers questions about the business's finances.
