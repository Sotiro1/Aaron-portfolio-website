AI automation at LDC Strategies

One day a week at an AI and web agency in Henley Beach, building n8n workflows that connect OpenAI, scrapers, Google Sheets, GoHighLevel and Slack, so leads are researched before a person ever looks at them.

Highlight

The main piece was a company research pipeline the team used in place of manual prospecting. When a lead comes in, the workflow finds their website with Serper, scrapes it with FireCrawl, and has GPT-4o-mini write a structured pre-call brief: industry, services, revenue signals, market position and pain points. The brief is pushed to GoHighLevel, posted to Slack and saved to Google Sheets before anyone has opened the lead.

I also compared self-hosted n8n setups to see whether the agency could run its own instance.
